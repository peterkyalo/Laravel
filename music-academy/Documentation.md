## 1. System Overview

Baritone Music Academy is a comprehensive Learning Management System (LMS) tailored specifically for musical education. It features:
- **Public Conservatory:** A sleek, mobile-friendly catalog to showcase masterclasses, faculty, and academy journals.
- **Secure Authentication:** Role-based access control separating Students, Instructors, and Administrators.
- **Interactive Classrooms:** A focused environment combining video lessons, digital sheet music, and theory assignments.
- **Grading & Certification:** Tools for instructors to provide audio/video critique, and an automated system that issues verifiable PDF diplomas upon course completion.

## 2. Getting Started (All Users)

### Registration & Login
1. **Public Site**: Navigate to the homepage to explore available Masterclasses, read the Academy Journal (Blog), or verify certificates.
2. **Account Creation**: Click the **Join Academy** button in the top right corner. Provide your basic information to register as a new student. 
3. **Log In**: Click the **Log In** button and enter your email and password.
4. **Dashboard Access**: Upon logging in, the system will automatically route you to your specific dashboard based on your assigned role (Student, Instructor, or Admin).

### Switching Views
- Use the **Public Site** button in the top navigation bar of your dashboard to seamlessly switch back to the public-facing pages at any time.

## 3. Student Guide

The Student Dashboard is your central hub for musical progression, coursework, and academy communication.

### Enrolling & Accessing Masterclasses
1. Navigate to **Explore Courses** to view the public catalog and enroll in new Masterclasses.
2. In your dashboard, go to **My Enrolled Masterclasses**.
3. Click on any course to enter the **Classroom View**. 
4. The Classroom provides a focused environment with a sidebar containing all your lessons, audio/video materials, and interactive digital scores.

### Practice Assignments & Quizzes
- **Assignments**: Instructors will assign practice etudes and assignments. Navigate to **My Practice Assignments** to view pending tasks. You can upload video/audio recordings of your practice here to receive grades and high-fidelity audio critique from your instructor.
- **Quizzes**: Test your music theory knowledge. Your scores are automatically logged in the system and contribute to your course completion status.

### Tuition & Payments
- Navigate to **My Tuition & Payments** to view pending invoices, past transaction history, and securely handle your tuition fees via the integrated payment gateways.

### Diplomas & Certificates
- Upon completing 100% of a Masterclass, you will automatically be awarded a verified certificate.
- Navigate to **My Diplomas & Certificates** to download your PDF certificate. 
- You can share the unique **Credential ID** with third parties, who can verify its authenticity on the public **Verify Certificate** page.

## 4. Instructor Guide

The Instructor Studio Dashboard equips you with the tools needed to manage your classes, grade students, and foster artistry.

### Curriculum Hub (Managing Courses)
1. Navigate to **Manage Masterclasses** to view courses assigned to you.
2. Click **Create New Masterclass** to start a new syllabus.
3. Structure your courses by adding **Lessons**. You can upload video demonstrations, embed external links, and attach sheet music PDFs.
4. Use the built-in rich text editor to format lesson content beautifully.

### Grading & Feedback
1. Navigate to **Assignments & Practice Submissions**.
2. Here, you will see a queue of student audio/video uploads.
3. Review the submissions, provide a numerical grade, and use the feedback box to deliver comprehensive, conservatory-level critiques to your students.

### Exam Management
- Create theory quizzes directly tied to your Masterclasses. Manage questions, set passing scores, and review student performance analytics to gauge class comprehension.

## 5. Administrator Guide

The Admin Conservatory Overview provides absolute control over the platform's infrastructure, users, and aesthetic presentation.

### User & Role Management
1. Navigate to **Manage Academy Users**.
2. You can view all registered users.
3. Invite new instructors, manage student enrollments, or promote users to different roles (e.g., upgrading a Student to an Instructor).

### Site Customizer (Dynamic Settings)
- Go to the **Site Customizer** under the Admin settings.
- This powerful tool allows you to dynamically update the platform's global settings without touching the code.
- **Branding**: Change the Site Name, Tagline, and Logos.
- **Contact Info**: Update the Academy's physical address, email, phone number, and Social Media Links.
- **Navigation Toggles**: Enable or disable specific menus (e.g., hiding the Blog or specific navigation links).

### Global Oversight
- **Tuition Management**: View all platform transactions, oversee simulated payment gateways, and manage bursar office requests.
- **Academy Bulletins (Announcements)**: Post global announcements that appear immediately on all users' dashboards.
- **Manage Instruments**: Add, edit, or remove instrument categories (e.g., Piano, Strings, Woodwinds, Vocals) to organize the course catalog dynamically.

## 6. Common Features

### Academy Inbox (Messaging)
- A built-in messaging system accessible via the **envelope icon** in the top right of your dashboard.
- Communicate directly with instructors, peers, or administration.
- Keep track of unread messages with the real-time red notification badge.

### Profile & Security
- Click your **Avatar** in the top right corner and select **Profile & Password**.
- Update your personal information, change your display picture, and update your secure password at any time.

*Always remember to use the **Log Out** button located in the profile dropdown menu when using a shared device.*
